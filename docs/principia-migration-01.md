# PRINCIPIA — migration registry 01

**Status:** ACTIVE EDITORIAL REGISTRY  
**Canonical target:** `PSI-R3-CONSOLIDATED-CANON-03`  
**Purpose:** prevent historical PSI/LOGOS/SOP material from being silently promoted into the current mathematical core.

This file is an editorial migration map, not a new mathematical module. It records what survives from the historical Principia family and in which role.

## 1. Migration states

Every migrated block receives one of four primary states:

- `KEEP` — content remains valid in its stated scope;
- `REFORMULATE` — useful content survives, but its claim strength, types, hypotheses or role must be rewritten;
- `SUPERSEDE` — later PSI architecture replaces the old claim as a statement of the core;
- `GENEALOGY` — preserve as intellectual / experimental history, not current canon.

Secondary placement:

- `V1` — Foundations;
- `V2` — PSI theorems;
- `V3` — Realizations / Models / Laboratories;
- `V4` — Genealogy.

The governing rule is:

\[
\boxed{\text{later typed canon} > \text{older analogy or field ontology}.}
\]

A historical claim is never deleted merely because it is superseded. It is moved to the layer where its actual evidential role remains legible.

## 2. Current target architecture

The current PSI core is contract-relative:

\[
\mathfrak P_c=(\Omega_c,\Psi_c,\mathcal K_c,\mathscr O_{\mathcal T,c},\delta_c).
\]

The central public objects are the compatible fiber, task closure, task equivalence and task quotient:

\[
F_c(Y)=\Psi_c^{-1}(\mathcal K_c^Y),
\qquad
E_{\mathcal T,c}=\bigcap_{R\in\mathscr R_{\mathcal T,c}}\ker_{\rm eq}R,
\qquad
M_{\mathcal T,c}=\Omega_c/E_{\mathcal T,c}.
\]

Exact task-level decidability is

\[
|q_{\mathcal T,c}(F_c(Y))|=1
\iff
F_c(Y)\neq\varnothing
\land
F_c(Y)^2\subseteq E_{\mathcal T,c}.
\]

Historical material is migrated relative to this architecture. It does not redefine it.

## 3. `Principia_Semantica.docx`

### 3.1 Model ψ and metrics `T/R/E/χ`

**Historical object.** Embedding trajectories were processed through numerical first/second differences and cosine-distance structure to produce heuristic metrics `T`, `R`, `E`, `χ`.

**Migration:** `KEEP + GENEALOGY` → `V3/V4`.

Keep:
- the computational pipeline as a historical realization;
- poetry / linguistic experiments as laboratories;
- concrete numerical procedures when reproducible.

Do not keep as core:
- identification of these four metrics with universal primitives of PSI;
- inference from the metric names alone to ontological torsion, curvature, energy or coherence.

Current reading: these are observables / representations chosen under a protocol. Their epistemic force must be assessed through the current observation/fiber/task architecture.

### 3.2 Multimodal model ψ

**Historical object.** Text/image/audio embeddings, cross-modal alignment, `χ_multi`, `Δψ`, synchronized trajectories and cultural/film experiments.

**Migration:** `REFORMULATE` → `V3`, with historical exposition in `V4`.

Preserve the experiments as actual objects. Replace claims of a universal multimodal semantic field by explicit statements of:

\[
\text{representation}
\to
\text{observation}
\to
\text{compatible interpretations}
\to
\text{task / test}.
\]

A film, poem or image remains a legitimate PSI laboratory; the field ontology is not required for that legitimacy.

### 3.3 STPψ / GTPψ and field ontology

**Historical object.** A field `ψ` on a semantic manifold, background fields, local field equations, SU(2)-style dynamics and geometrical language of meaning.

**Migration:** `SUPERSEDE + GENEALOGY` → `V4`; selected realizational mathematics may migrate to `V3` after independent typing.

Reason: current PSI is a theory/program of identifiability and justified inference under limited observation, not an ontology of meaning. Field language can survive as a model realization only when its objects, domains and observables are specified independently.

## 4. `PRINCIPIA_SEMANTICA_INTEGRATA.pdf`

### 4.1 Separation of ontology and representation

**Migration:** `KEEP / REFORMULATE` → `V1`.

The older text already warns that field realizations are models of representation rather than literal physical or consciousness fields. Preserve this methodological restriction, but express it in current language:

\[
\text{representation} \neq \text{represented object}.
\]

This is compatible with the present PSI discipline that observation is not the hidden object.

### 4.2 Epistemic limitations and anti-reification warnings

**Migration:** `KEEP` → `V1` and editorial policy.

Preserve especially:
- dependence on embedding choice;
- corpus / modality bias;
- prohibition against equating metric value with semantic or normative value;
- warning against automating human interpretation by rhetorical substitution;
- dual-use warning for propaganda / influence optimization.

These are stronger today, not weaker.

### 4.3 TAO/SMOK → STPψ/GTPψ → Model ψ hierarchy

**Migration:** `SUPERSEDE + GENEALOGY` → `V4`.

It is historically important because it shows how PSI reached the later observation/fiber architecture. It is not the present hierarchy of the mathematical core.

### 4.4 Strong claims about measuring or verifying “sense”

**Migration:** `REFORMULATE`.

Replace statements of the form

`meaning is measured / verified by T,R,E,χ`

with protocol-relative statements such as

`the chosen representation and observables separate specified classes under the stated dataset / task / validation procedure`.

No metric is promoted from a realization to the ontology of the measured object merely by empirical correlation.

## 5. `PRINCIPIA_SEMANTICA_UNIVERSALIA.pdf`

### 5.1 Genomic field Λ

**Migration:** `KEEP as representation` → `V3`.

The document explicitly treats `Λ(x)` as a constructed representational function of genomic/epigenetic signals rather than an ontological property of DNA. This is compatible with current PSI provided the observation contract and preprocessing provenance are explicit.

### 5.2 SOP-11b reproducible pipeline

**Migration:** `KEEP` → `V3`.

Preserve:
- immutable raw-data layer;
- explicit transformation DAG;
- materialized intermediate data;
- run manifests and parameter logging;
- comparison against null/surrogate models;
- multiple-testing controls where correctly specified.

This is valuable as experimental/provenance discipline independently of the older semantic interpretation.

### 5.3 Golden-ratio resonance model and fixed thresholds

**Migration:** `REFORMULATE / OPEN` → `V3`.

The model may remain a pre-specified alternative hypothesis. Claims such as fixed `ΔAIC`, `LA` or golden-ratio thresholds proving a universal structural resonance require independent statistical justification for the stated sampling model and test family.

Do not infer:

\[
\text{better fit of a selected model}
\Rightarrow
\text{ontological resonance}.
\]

### 5.4 Strong asymptotic / error-control claims

**Migration:** `REFORMULATE`, with unsupported versions excluded from theorem status.

Claims such as
- a fixed threshold guaranteeing a universal `α < 0.01`;
- exponential false-positive reduction from a fixed LA threshold;
- asymptotic consistency of the complete SOP-11b procedure;

must be restated with the precise stochastic model, dependence assumptions, selection procedure and proof. Until that exists, they are not current PSI theorems.

## 6. PHISICA / LOGOS material

PHISICA and LOGOS are retained as realizations / mathematical laboratories, not as replacements for CORE5.

**Migration pattern:**

- legitimate operator reductions with explicit domains / regularity → `KEEP` in `V3`;
- formal calculations without domain hypotheses → `REFORMULATE`;
- field-language interpretations presented as universal PSI primitives → `GENEALOGY`;
- imported isoparametric, Sturm–Liouville, spectral or pseudospectral mathematics → `CLASSICAL` provenance + `ADAPTED` or `BENCHMARK` role.

The standing rule for unbounded operators remains:

\[
\boxed{\text{domain before formal algebra}.}
\]

## 9. Redaction order

The new Principia should not be produced by editing the old books linearly.

Use:

\[
\boxed{
\text{canonical claim registry}
\to
\text{Volume I/II skeleton}
\to
\text{realizations/labs}
\to
\text{genealogy}
}
\]

Operationally:

1. migrate only claims needed by current CORE5 and its proved consequences;
2. state imported classical mathematics with provenance;
3. move realizations and laboratories after the theorem layer;
4. retain superseded material in genealogy rather than silently deleting it;
5. never promote an old empirical metaphor because it has persuasive terminology;
6. record every later change against this registry.

## 10. Immediate editorial gates

Before a historical block enters the new Principia, require:

`G1 TYPE` — object, domain/codomain and contract are explicit.  
`G2 STATUS` — claim status and role in PSI are separate.  
`G3 SOURCE` — imported mathematics / empirical data have recoverable provenance.  
`G4 PROOF/TEST` — theorem has proof; empirical claim has a stated protocol and actual evidence.  
`G5 CANON` — no conflict with `PSI-R3-CONSOLIDATED-CANON-03`.  
`G6 DUAL-USE` — publication state is `PUBLIC | REVIEW | WITHHOLD` where relevant.

Only blocks passing the relevant gates enter Volumes I–III as current material. Everything else remains `OPEN`, `SUPERSEDED`, or `GENEALOGY`.
