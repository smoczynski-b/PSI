# PSI — public source and disclosure governance

**Status:** PUBLIC EDITORIAL / PROVENANCE POLICY

This document is not a mathematical primitive.

## Provenance

A nontrivial public claim should preserve its source or canonical parent, proposition, transformation/derivation path, dependencies and epistemic status. Historical material is evidence of what the project once claimed; it is not automatically a current theorem.

## Processing order

\[
\boxed{\text{SOURCE}\to\text{ANALYSIS}\to\text{AUDIT}\to\text{VERDICT}\to\text{PLACEMENT}\to\text{DISCLOSURE}\to\text{TRANSMISSION}.}
\]

`KEEP | REFORMULATE | SUPERSEDE | GENEALOGY` concerns placement. `PUBLIC | REVIEW | WITHHOLD` concerns disclosure. These axes are independent.

A `PUBLIC` decision must identify the exact file/fragment scope. Authorization to audit, repair, test or store material is not automatically authorization to publish its source material.

## Drift test

Before migration or redaction ask whether the object, type/domain, hypotheses, claim strength, analogy/identity, observable/core role, causal status or theorem/model status changed. Any unrecorded material change blocks automatic promotion.

## Public defaults

Conversation exports, private agent instructions, telemetry, account data, credentials and unpublished drafts default to `REVIEW` or `WITHHOLD`. Public scientific value does not override disclosure control.

## Bronsztejn gate

\[
\boxed{\text{object}\to\text{type/domain}\to\text{conditions}\to\text{quantity}\to\text{proof/test}.}
\]

Missing elements block promotion; they do not by themselves prove falsity.
