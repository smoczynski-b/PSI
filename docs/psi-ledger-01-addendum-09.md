# A. Epistemic updates

## E060 — P9 working material contained structural contradictions

Later working syntheses mixed the energy Hessian with the dissipative Hermitian part and raw resolvent sensitivity with the Kreiss constant.

Two explicit invalid claims were identified:

1. `[H,N]=0` together with nonnormality in a decomposition with H self-adjoint and N skew-adjoint;
2. `K(-mu I) -> infinity` as mu -> 0 under the standard continuous-time Kreiss definition.

Both are withdrawn.

## E061 — positive dissipative Hermitian part excludes transient growth in the same norm

For

\[
A=-H+N,
\quad H\ge\mu I,
\quad N^*=-N,
\]

we have

\[
\|e^{tA}\|\le e^{-\mu t}
\]

and

\[
\mathcal K(A)=1.
\]

Thus the old Jordan transient example is not in this class once transient growth occurs.

## E062 — metric-gradient sector yields a valid restricted bridge

For

\[
A=-G^{-1}H,
\quad G,H>0,
\]

with

\[
B=G^{-1/2}HG^{-1/2},
\]

## E063 — transient growth is norm relative

An explicit positive-definite 2x2 example has strict contraction in the energy norm and positive Euclidean numerical abscissa. Thus Euclidean transient growth can be generated by metric mismatch even in a genuine gradient system.

## E064 — P9 general remains OPEN / CENTRAL

The migration closes only P9-G and the local Jordan laboratory. It does not settle unrestricted nonnormal generators, hypocoercive degeneracy, continuous spectrum or infinite-dimensional resolvent-to-growth bounds.
